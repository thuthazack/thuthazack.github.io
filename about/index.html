About

Welcome! My name is Thuta Aung, but you can call me Zack. I graduated from the University of Michigan with a BSE in computer engineering. My primary area of focus is firmware and low-level software for low-power embedded systems.

I have worked on embedded sensing projects, including the Smart Sensor Interface and a wearable posture-feedback system. I also cofounded BridgeBurma, a community focused on sharing opportunities and knowledge among Burmese students around the world.

Explore my CV and writing, or browse the projects on the home page.
